### What a question looks like

Wrong answers are not drawn at random. They are the concepts you would actually
confuse, so getting one wrong tells you which neighbour you reached for.

<div class="ed-card">
  <span class="ed-key">what does it claim &middot; micro / externalities</span>
  <p><strong>What does the Coase theorem actually claim?</strong></p>
  <div class="ed-opt right">With well-defined property rights and zero transaction costs, private bargaining reaches the efficient allocation regardless of who initially holds the rights; the initial assignment determines only the distribution of surplus.<br><span class="ed-key">correct</span></div>
  <div class="ed-opt ">No bargaining mechanism can be simultaneously efficient, incentive compatible, individually rational and budget balanced when both parties have private values with overlapping supports.</div>
  <div class="ed-opt chosen">A tax set equal to the marginal external damage at the efficient quantity, which internalises a negative externality by making private marginal cost equal social marginal cost.<br><span class="ed-key">what I picked</span></div>
  <div class="ed-opt ">Overuse of a rival, non-excludable resource because each user bears only a fraction of the congestion cost their use imposes on others.</div>
</div>

<div class="ed-card">
  <span class="ed-key">why that was wrong</span>
  <p>That is the <strong>Pigouvian tax</strong>: A tax set equal to the marginal external damage at the efficient quantity, which internalises a negative externality by making private marginal cost equal social marginal cost.</p>
